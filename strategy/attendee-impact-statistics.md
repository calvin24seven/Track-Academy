# Attendee impact statistics for communications

Use this copy when updating press releases and other public communications. The same figures are maintained in `website/src/data/impact.ts` and displayed on the homepage, Impact page and Protect Brent Athletics campaign page.

## Press release paragraph

Track Academy supported 519 young people in the past year. Of those young people, 64% were from low-income families, 29% had a disability or long-term health condition, 58% were girls, and 90% were from Black, Asian, mixed and other minoritised ethnic backgrounds.

Keep the reporting period as “the past year” unless Track Academy confirms the exact dates. Historical figures in the 2024 Annual Report should remain clearly labelled as 2024.
